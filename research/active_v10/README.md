# Active V10 — V8/V4 separate-sleeve capital allocation

V10 does not introduce a new trading signal. It tests whether the already frozen Active V4 low-turnover spot trend process can reduce the risk of the frozen Active V8 growth candidate without destroying its return profile.

## Frozen construction

- 80% of initial capital: Active V8 ratchet account;
- 20% of initial capital: exact Active V4 fixed-risk account;
- accounts are separate and never cross-fund each other;
- portfolio equity is their sum;
- V8 retains its 1.50 → 1.00 → 0.75 irreversible ratchet and 0.85 target gross cap;
- V4 uses its exact frozen signal and no leverage;
- both sleeves use V8 stress/severe spot cost assumptions (40/80 bps per side);
- V8 perpetual costs are 40/80 bps per side;
- forced spot delistings retain the 100 bps penalty.

## Selection

Weights 20%, 30%, 40% and 50% V4 were ranked only on 2021–2025. A weight was eligible only when all stress periods were positive, the worst severe return was above -10%, worst severe drawdown above -30%, median stress annualized return at least 85% of V8, and annual turnover below 31x. The 20% weight was the only eligible separate-sleeve allocation.

January–June 2026 was opened only after the weight was frozen. It is not pristine at the overall research-program level because that period had been seen by older experiments.

This candidate is a balanced paper-forward tier, not permission for live trading.
